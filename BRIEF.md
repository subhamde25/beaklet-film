## Intent

Cinematic 45s vertical brand film for Beaklet (test assignment from the Beaklet brief doc).
User asked for a "cinematic product ad", premium/dark look like the Rotoris reference reels
(rotoris.world posts DON3ZA4kh-o and DScSd7bEp4j), good font, text pop-ups, motion graphics,
high-level SFX, a deep voice from Fish Audio, and their SD watermark.

Concept: SCREEN vs INK. Type opens in a typed monospace (the screen), and the film
gradually hands the voice to an ink-like italic serif (the pen). The hornbill is drawn as
2D gold line art and match-cut into the real beak-shaped clip.
